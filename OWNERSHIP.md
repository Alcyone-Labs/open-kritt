in writing, copyright in open·kritt is held jointly by Harel Rom and Gabriel Balko.

Contributions are accepted only after the contributor executes a separate written
copyright assignment transferring copyright in the contribution jointly to Harel Rom
and Gabriel Balko. A Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off confirms provenance and
permission to contribute, but is not itself a copyright assignment. See
[the contribution ownership process](legal/README.md) and
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Submitting a contribution does not confer any ownership interest in open·kritt. Until
an assignment is validly executed, the contributor may own copyright in their original
work, so the contribution must not be merged. After an effective assignment, the
contributor retains no transferable economic copyright ownership in the assigned work.
They retain the same AGPL permissions as every recipient and any authorship or moral
rights that applicable law does not permit them to assign or waive. The assignment must
include appropriate waiver, consent, and non-assertion terms to the fullest extent the
applicable law permits.
