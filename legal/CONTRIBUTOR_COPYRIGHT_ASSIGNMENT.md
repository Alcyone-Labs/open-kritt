# open·kritt Contributor Copyright Assignment Agreement

> **Legal-review status:** This template has not yet been approved by Israeli or U.S.
> counsel. Do not treat an unsigned copy, pull-request submission, or repository checkbox
> as an executed assignment.

This Contributor Copyright Assignment Agreement (the **Agreement**) is between the
person or entity identified as **Contributor** below and Harel Rom and Gabriel Balko,
each acting personally and together referred to as the **Owners**. The Agreement becomes
effective only when signed by the Contributor and both Owners.

## 1. Definitions

**Contribution** means original software, documentation, artwork, or other copyrightable
material that the Contributor owns and intentionally submits to the official open·kritt
repository, including the past contributions specifically identified in the signature
schedule and future submissions made using a GitHub identity listed there. Material
clearly marked in writing as "Not a Contribution" is excluded.

**Copyright** means all copyright, neighboring rights, and other transferable rights in
a work of authorship, together with renewals, extensions, registrations, applications,
and accrued claims, throughout the world and for their full duration.

**Project** means the official open·kritt project and canonical repository maintained at
`https://github.com/Kritt-ai/open-kritt`.

## 2. Copyright assignment

The Contributor assigns to the Owners, in equal undivided shares, all right, title, and
interest in Copyright covering each Contribution. For a future Contribution, the
assignment takes effect immediately when the Contribution is created and submitted to
the Project, to the maximum extent applicable law permits an assignment of future
rights.

The assignment includes the exclusive rights to reproduce, modify, prepare derivative
works, distribute, display, perform, make available, license, sublicense, relicense,
register, enforce, settle claims concerning, and recover damages for the Contribution.

To the extent a right cannot be assigned, the Contributor grants the Owners an
exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free
license to exercise that right. To the extent a right can be neither assigned nor
licensed, the Contributor waives it and agrees not to assert it against the Owners, the
Project, their successors, or direct and indirect licensees, to the maximum extent
applicable law permits.

The Contributor retains no ownership or governance interest in the Project by reason of
making a Contribution. This Agreement does not prevent the Contributor from exercising
rights received under the license governing a publicly released version of the Project.

## 3. Moral rights

To the maximum extent applicable law permits, the Contributor waives and agrees not to
assert moral rights relating to a Contribution. Where waiver is unavailable, the
Contributor consents to the Owners and their licensees using, modifying, combining,
publishing, omitting attribution for, and otherwise exercising Copyright in the
Contribution, subject to any consent that applicable law does not permit the Contributor
to give in advance.

## 4. Patent license

The Contributor grants the Owners a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, transferable,
sublicensable, royalty-free patent license to make, have made, use, offer, sell, import,
and otherwise transfer the Contribution alone or combined with the Project. This license
covers only patent claims the Contributor owns or controls that are necessarily
infringed by the Contribution or its combination with the Project as submitted.

## 5. Contributor promises

The Contributor represents that:

- the Contributor has legal authority to enter into and perform this Agreement;
- the Contributor owns the rights being assigned or licensed;
- the Contribution is original except for third-party material clearly identified in
  writing, with its source and license;
- this Agreement does not violate an obligation to an employer, client, university, or
  other party; and
- any entity that could own or control the Contribution has signed an employer or entity
  disclaimer acceptable to the Owners.

The Contributor will promptly disclose any fact that makes these representations
incorrect and will sign reasonable additional documents needed to confirm or record the
rights granted by this Agreement.

## 6. Project licensing and acceptance

The Owners may accept, reject, remove, modify, enforce, and license a Contribution at
their discretion, including under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or other
terms selected by the Owners. The Owners are not required to merge or continue using a
Contribution. No payment is owed for a Contribution unless all parties separately agree
in writing.

## 7. General terms

This Agreement is the entire agreement concerning ownership of the Contributions it
covers and may be amended only in a writing signed by the Contributor and both Owners.
If a provision is unenforceable, it will be limited to the minimum extent necessary and
the remaining provisions will continue in effect. Electronic signatures and counterparts
are permitted.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Israel, excluding conflict-of-law
rules. The competent courts located in Tel Aviv-Jaffa, Israel have exclusive jurisdiction,
subject to intellectual-property rules that applicable law does not permit the parties
to alter by contract.
